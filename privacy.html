2. Information we collect

We aim to collect only the information needed to run, improve and support our Games. Depending on the Game and the features you use, this may include:

Information collected automatically

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system and version, language, time zone and screen settings.
  • Identifiers, such as an app-specific or installation identifier, and, where permitted and where you have given any required consent, mobile advertising identifiers (for example, Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID).
  • Gameplay and usage data, such as levels played, progress, session length, in-game events and settings.
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash reports and performance data.
  • Approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Information you provide

  • Support communications, such as your email address and the content of messages you send us.
  • Purchase information, if a Game offers in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your full payment card details; we may receive confirmation of a purchase and related transaction information.

Our Games do not require you to create an account with us, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or contacts.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the Games, including saving your progress and settings.
  • To understand how the Games are used and to fix bugs, improve performance and develop new features.
  • To show advertising, where a Game includes ads, and to measure ad performance.
  • To process and verify in-app purchases.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse and security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

Where the law requires a legal basis for processing (for example, in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), we rely on performance of a contract, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Services, your consent (for example, for personalized advertising where required), and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services and SDKs

Our Games may use third-party software development kits (SDKs) and services that collect information directly from your device, under their own privacy policies. These may include:

  • Analytics providers, which help us understand gameplay and usage.
  • Crash reporting and performance tools, which help us find and fix technical problems.
  • Advertising networks and mediation platforms, which may display ads and use device identifiers and usage data to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads.
  • Attribution and measurement providers, which help us understand how players discover our Games.
  • Platform services from Apple and Google, such as app distribution and in-app purchase processing.

The specific third-party services used by a Game will be described in that Game's store listing privacy details (such as the App Store privacy label or the Google Play Data safety section) and, where required, in an in-game notice. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties.

On iOS, we will only access the advertising identifier for tracking if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before personalized advertising is shown.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We may share information:

  • With service providers and SDK partners that perform services for us, as described above.
  • With advertising partners, where a Game includes ads. Under some U.S. state laws, this may be considered "sharing" or "selling" for targeted advertising; see Your choices and rights.
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process or a valid government request.
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of RED MOD DIGITAL LLC, our players or others.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising identifiers: you can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Privacy / Google › Ads).
  • Uninstalling: you can stop all collection of information by a Game by uninstalling it.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or U.S. state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@redmoddigital.com. We may need to verify your request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Data deletion requests

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@redmoddigital.com with the subject line "Data deletion request". Please include:

  • the name of the Game,
  • your device platform (iOS or Android), and
  • if available, your in-game player ID (often shown in the Game's settings screen).

We will confirm receipt and process your request within 30 days, unless the law allows or requires a longer period. Some information may be kept where we are legally required to do so (for example, transaction records). Deleting your data may reset your game progress and cannot be undone. Data held by third-party SDK providers can also be deleted by contacting those providers directly.

9. Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or a higher minimum age where required by local law, such as 16 in some countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without verified parental consent where required, we will delete it as soon as possible.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@redmoddigital.com and we will take steps to delete it. If a future Game is designed for children, it will follow applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA) and the relevant App Store and Google Play family policies, and any specific practices will be described in that Game's store listing.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

RED MOD DIGITAL LLC is based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for transfers of personal information outside your country.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above. If changes are significant, we may also notify you within the Games. Your continued use of the Services after an update means you accept the revised policy.
