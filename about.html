About us

A young studio, focused on mobile.

RED MOD DIGITAL LLC was formed in Delaware, USA, on December 3, 2025, to make free-to-play mobile games that are simple to learn, satisfying to master and fair to play.

Our story

RED MOD is a young, independent game studio. Semih Okur founded the company as a limited liability company in the State of Delaware in December 2025, with a simple plan: build a small, capable team and focus on mobile games that people enjoy picking up every day.

Today we are a team of four designers and developers working in Unity. We handle the full life of our games ourselves: design, development, balancing, publishing on the App Store and Google Play, and live content updates after launch.

Our current project is PUZZLESCREW, a casual screw puzzle game in development for iOS and Android.

Our mission

We want to make mobile games that respect players' time, attention and trust. Free-to-play games work best when players feel in control, so our games are free to download and play, supported by ads and optional in-app purchases, and we aim to make every level fair, clear and worth finishing.

What we value

Players first

We design for the person holding the phone. Clear rules, fair difficulty and honest monetization come before short-term numbers.

Small and focused

A team of four can move quickly. We keep scope tight, make decisions fast and put our energy into the games themselves.

Polish matters

Small details make a game feel good: responsive controls, readable screens and smooth performance on everyday devices.

Open and accountable

We publish clear privacy and terms documents, answer support requests ourselves and keep our company details public.

Our team

RED MOD is a team of four designers and developers. We work closely together across game design, Unity development, balancing and live content, so ideas move from discussion to a playable build quickly.
