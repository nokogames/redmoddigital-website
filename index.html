What we do

We take a mobile game from first idea to live product, and we stay with it after launch. Our focus is free-to-play games that are easy to learn, satisfying to master and comfortable to play in short sessions on a phone.

Game design

We start from one clear core mechanic, then build levels, progression and rewards around it so every session has a goal and a satisfying finish.

Development in Unity

Our games are built with Unity, which lets us maintain one codebase for both iOS and Android while paying close attention to performance on everyday phones.

Publishing

We publish our own games on the App Store and Google Play and manage the store listings, privacy disclosures and player support ourselves.

Balancing & live updates

After launch we study how levels are played, rebalance difficulty where players get stuck and add fresh content through regular updates.

How we work

Our process is short and practical. An idea earns its place by being fun on a real phone, so we test early, change things quickly and keep the player at the centre of every decision.

  1. Prototype

    We turn an idea into a small playable build in Unity as early as possible, so the team can judge the core mechanic by playing it instead of describing it.

  2. Playtest

    We play every build on iOS and Android devices, watch where players hesitate or get stuck, and adjust rules, controls and difficulty based on what we see.

  3. Polish

    Once the core loop works, we refine the details: animation, sound, feedback, level pacing and performance, until the game feels clean and responsive.

  4. Launch & live updates

    We publish on the App Store and Google Play, then keep the game fresh with balancing passes, fixes and new content delivered through regular updates.
