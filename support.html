What to include in your message

A complete first message helps us solve your issue without back-and-forth. Please include:

  • The game you are playing, for example PUZZLESCREW.
  • Your device model, for example the exact iPhone or Android phone model.
  • Your operating system version (iOS or Android version).
  • The game version. On iOS: Settings › General › iPhone Storage › the game. On Android: Settings › Apps › the game.
  • A short description of the problem and the steps that led to it.
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if you can.
  • For purchase questions, the order number from your Apple or Google Play receipt.

Response time: we reply within 2–3 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free to play?

Yes. Our games are free to download and play. They are supported by ads and optional in-app purchases. Purchases are never required to play.

How is my progress saved, and how do I restore purchases?

Progress. Our games do not require you to create an account. Your progress and settings are saved by the game on your device, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data can reset your progress.

Restoring purchases. In-app purchases are linked to the Apple ID or Google account you used to buy them. If a purchase is missing after reinstalling the game or moving to a new device:

  • make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the purchase;
  • use the game's restore purchases option, where the game offers one; and
  • if the purchase still does not appear, email us with the order number from your receipt and we will look into it.
How do I get a refund?

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google, not by RED MOD, so refunds are handled under their policies. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

  • App Store (iPhone and iPad): request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play (Android): follow Google's instructions at support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637.

If you have trouble with a purchase that the store cannot solve, email us and we will help where we can.

How do I report a bug?

Email info@redmoddigital.com with the subject line "Bug report". Tell us your device model, operating system version and game version, describe what you were doing when the problem happened, and attach screenshots or a screen recording if possible. Clear reports help us reproduce and fix issues faster.

How do I delete my data?

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@redmoddigital.com with the subject line "Data deletion request". Please include:

  • the name of the game,
  • your device platform (iOS or Android), and
  • if available, your in-game player ID (often shown in the game's settings screen).

We will confirm receipt and process your request within 30 days, unless the law allows or requires a longer period. Some information may be kept where we are legally required to do so (for example, transaction records). Deleting your data may reset your game progress and cannot be undone. Data held by third-party SDK providers can also be deleted by contacting those providers directly.

The full procedure is described in section 8 of our Privacy Policy.

How do ads work, and how can I manage my privacy choices?

Our games are free to play and supported by ads. Ads may be delivered by third-party advertising networks, which may use device identifiers and usage data to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads.

  • iOS: we only access the advertising identifier for tracking if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in Settings › Privacy / Google › Ads.
  • Consent: where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
  • Uninstalling a game stops all collection of information by that game.

Read more in Third-party services and SDKs and Your choices and rights in our Privacy Policy.
