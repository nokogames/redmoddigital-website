PUZZLESCREW

Unscrew the bolts, clear the board and reveal the picture hidden underneath. A casual screw puzzle game by RED MOD DIGITAL LLC for iOS and Android.

How to play Player support

About the game

PUZZLESCREW is a casual unscrew puzzle. Each level is a board of colourful plates held in place by bolts. Your job is to work out the right order to unscrew them, free the plates and clear the board.

Every bolt you collect also uncovers a piece of a hidden picture. The image builds up tile by tile as you play, and finishing the level reveals the full photo. That gives every move a small reward and every level a clear goal.

The pictures belong to collectible lifestyle photo albums set in everyday places, from a beach holiday to a night out in the city. Each album holds ten levels, and completing all ten finishes the collection.

How to play

The rules take seconds to learn. The challenge grows as each new board adds more bolts and plates.

  1. Study the board

    Look at how the plates overlap and which bolts hold each plate in place before you make a move.

  2. Unscrew in order

    Tap a bolt to unscrew it. Choose your order carefully to free the plates one by one and clear the board.

  3. Reveal the picture

    Every bolt you collect uncovers another piece of the hidden picture behind the board.

  4. Complete the album

    Finish the level to see the full photo, then move on. Every tenth level is an album finale.
